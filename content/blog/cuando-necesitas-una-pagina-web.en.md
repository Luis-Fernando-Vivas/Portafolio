In Colombia, thousands of companies and startups are growing fueled by social media and messaging apps. In fact, recent studies on the country's business landscape show that 87% of MSMEs use WhatsApp as their regular sales channel, and more than half maintain an active presence on social media; however, 51% struggle to turn that visibility into actual sales. At the same time, reports from the Colombian Chamber of Electronic Commerce (CCCE, by its Spanish acronym) reveal that digital commerce surpassed 145 trillion Colombian pesos in annual sales, confirming that consumers shop online more frequently, but prioritize formal, agile, and secure channels. The big question for your business isn't whether you should have an online presence, but rather when the exact moment arrives when operating solely through chat apps falls short.

## 1. When answering messages becomes your biggest bottleneck

When starting out, handling every inquiry manually via chat works well because it lets you understand your buyers' questions and needs firsthand. However, as demand grows, this dynamic often turns into an operational trap. If your team spends hours sending the same PDF catalog, clarifying prices over and over, or manually verifying bank transfer receipts, your business has hit the ceiling of that channel.

A website eliminates this friction because it acts as a digital storefront open 24/7. Your customers can review product or service features, check pricing, and make buying decisions at their own pace—without having to wait for someone to be available right away to reply to a message.

## 2. When you need to build trust and protect your brand

Relying entirely on third-party platforms for your commercial operation brings significant risks. An algorithm tweak, a temporarily suspended account, or overwhelmed messaging channels can halt your revenue without warning. Furthermore, when potential clients look for professional services or higher-ticket products, they usually search Google to verify the company's credibility and track record.

> "Being on social media helps people discover your business, but having your own website is what formalizes your brand and puts you in control of your sales."

Owning your domain provides institutional backing, protects your company's reputation, and ensures you have a digital asset that truly belongs to your business, without depending on external policies or unexpected drops in reach.

## 3. Clear signs you've outgrown chat and need a website

Not every business requires a website from day one, but there are specific moments when lacking one causes you to lose sales opportunities:

- **You provide services and need to manage appointments:** if you spend valuable time coordinating meeting times, consulting sessions, or appointments back and forth over messages, a portal with built-in booking automates your entire schedule.
- **You're losing customers outside business hours:** if leads reach out at night or on weekends and go cold before you can reply the next day.
- **You want to streamline online payments:** integrating payment buttons and gateways to accept cards, bank transfers, or interoperable payment systems without asking for screenshots of receipts speeds up cash flow.
- **You want to appear in local search results:** when users search for providers in your industry on Google, an optimized website puts you right in front of people with genuine buying intent who are ready to close the deal.

## What you can do today to take the leap

Building a website doesn't require endless development cycles or runaway budgets. The best approach for a small or medium-sized business is to start with a fast, clearly messaged website designed specifically to turn visitors into customers. The key is ensuring it integrates with your day-to-day tools: your WhatsApp, your payment methods, and automated solutions for capturing leads.
