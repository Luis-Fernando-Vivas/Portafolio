Since September 2025, **Google's AI Mode** has been available in Spanish in Colombia and the rest of Latin America. It is a tab inside the search engine where, instead of a list of links, **Gemini writes a complete answer with cited sources** and lets you keep asking questions like in a chat. It also accepts questions by voice or with a photo.

For business owners, the question is obvious: if Google answers directly, who is going to visit my website?

## How it works under the hood

AI Mode uses a technique Google calls **query fan-out**: it splits the user's question into several subtopics, runs searches in parallel and builds a single answer from what it finds. At the end, it shows the sites it used as sources.

That means two things:

- **There will be fewer "drive-by" clicks.** Simple questions get answered without leaving Google.
- **The clicks you do get are worth more.** Someone who comes in from a cited source already knows the basics and is looking to hire, compare or buy.

The goal is no longer just "ranking first", but **being one of the sources the AI chooses to cite**.

## Five tweaks for your website

1. **Answer real questions, straight to the point.** An FAQ section with clear two- or three-sentence answers is exactly the kind of content AI can cite.
2. **Say what you do, where and for whom.** Services, city, reference prices and opening hours in text, not just in images.
3. **Use structured data.** Schema.org markup (business, services, FAQs, articles) tells Google exactly what each thing is.
4. **Show your expertise.** Real cases, figures, testimonials and visible authors: Google favors trustworthy sources.
5. **Mind speed and mobile.** A slow site, or one that is hard to read on a phone, loses to the competition, with or without AI.

> A website is no longer just a brochure for people: it is also the source machines learn about your business from.
