On October 6, 2026, Banco de la República (Colombia's central bank) released the official results from the first year of **Bre-B**, Colombia's instant and interoperable payments system. The figures show that Colombian shopping habits have changed for good: the network already exceeds **116 million registered keys (llaves)**, connects more than **36 million users**, and has processed over **one billion transactions**. With **245 participating financial entities** (including banks, credit unions, and digital wallets), interoperability grew 14-fold in just twelve months, directly transforming how merchants and companies collect payments for their products and services.

## What the Numbers Reveal About Purchasing Habits

The central bank's report highlights very clear patterns you should keep in mind when structuring your sales and collection channels:

* **Small-ticket payments are now digital:** 62% of all transactions made via Bre-B were for amounts under 50,000 Colombian pesos, confirming that cash continues to lose ground even for everyday pocket-change purchases.
* **QR codes lead the way:** 37% of transactions were completed by scanning a QR code, cementing its place as shoppers' preferred method for paying quickly without typing complex details.
* **Zero friction between financial institutions:** The old obstacle of "I can't buy from you because I use a different bank" has vanished. Today, transfers between accounts across different institutions clear in seconds and are available 24/7.
* **Peak shopping hours:** The highest transaction volume was concentrated between 6:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m., a key insight when scheduling marketing campaigns and staffing customer service.

> "During its first year, Bre-B supported more than one billion everyday transactions across 245 connected institutions, establishing instant, secure transfers regardless of the user's financial entity."

## What This Milestone Means for Your Business

If you are still managing collections by asking customers to send payment receipts via WhatsApp so someone can manually cross-reference them against a bank statement, your operation is losing time, money, and conversions.

The massive success of instant payments means consumers in Colombia are now accustomed to immediate fulfillment. When a buyer runs into friction, delays, or manual verification steps at an online checkout, an in-person store, or a service quote, they will simply turn to a competitor.

Furthermore, the 37% share of QR-based payments proves that the user experience must be frictionless both at the counter and on your website. Integrating standardized keys and QR codes into your quotes, payment links, and digital checkout flows is no longer an optional technical perk—it is the baseline expectation of today's consumer.

## Practical Steps to Prepare Your Payment Channels

To make the most of this shift and avoid falling behind, consider taking these practical steps in your business:

1. **Standardize your business Bre-B Key:** Make sure your company's key is registered with your primary financial institution and actively display it on your quotes, invoices, emails, and digital profiles.
2. **Enable QR payments across your website and physical locations:** Allow customers to scan and pay instantly from any banking app or mobile wallet.
3. **Automate sales reconciliation:** Connect your payment gateways and channels to your accounting software or CRM so every confirmed payment automatically updates inventory, issues electronic invoices, and notifies the customer without manual intervention.
4. **Optimize support during peak hours:** If your business sells online, strengthen customer support and automated responses between 5:00 p.m. and 8:00 p.m., when purchasing intent peaks.
