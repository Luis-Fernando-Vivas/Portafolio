## Manual work is your bottleneck

If you answer the same questions all day, copy data from chats into spreadsheets or chase payments one by one, your business depends on you being there. Automation doesn't replace your team: it takes away the tasks that don't need human judgment.

## What we automate

- **Appointment scheduling**: bookings that confirm themselves, land on your calendar and send reminders.
- **Documents and approvals**: quotes, invoices and contracts that are generated and routed without email chains.
- **Billing and payments**: payment gateways connected to your accounting, with automatic reconciliation.
- **Inventory**: stock synced across your online store, point of sale and spreadsheets.
- **AI customer service**: an agent on WhatsApp or your website that answers, qualifies and books leads 24/7, and hands off to a person when needed.
- **Real-time reporting**: dashboards that show sales, customers and operations at a glance.

## How we work

1. **Process discovery**: we map how the task is done today, step by step.
2. **Workflow design**: what gets automated, what stays with your team and how success is measured.
3. **Implementation**, connecting your tools through their APIs.
4. **Testing and documentation**, so you know exactly what each workflow does.
5. **Support and continuous improvement**.
