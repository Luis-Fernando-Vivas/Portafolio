# Automind

> Automind is a web design, web development, business process automation and AI agency based in Neiva, Huila, Colombia. It serves small and mid-sized businesses in Colombia, the United States and Canada, works remotely, publishes its prices in US dollars and builds sites in Spanish, English and French.

## Services

- Custom website design and development (landing pages, portfolios, multi-page sites)
- E-commerce (Shopify, WooCommerce, custom) with payment gateways (card, PSE, transfers, Stripe/PayPal)
- Business process automation: scheduling, quotes and invoices, payments and reconciliation, inventory, task coordination, real-time dashboards
- Integrations: CRMs, WhatsApp Business, email, spreadsheets, payment gateways, internal APIs
- AI agents for WhatsApp and websites that answer, qualify and book customers 24/7 with human handoff
- Branding and visual identity; digital marketing; custom apps
